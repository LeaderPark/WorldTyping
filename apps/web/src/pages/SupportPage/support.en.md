# Support

Here are answers to common questions about using TypeTrip. If you can't find what you're
looking for below, feel free to reach out any time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I sign in?

You can play Single Play and the Daily Challenge 100% without signing in. Signing in with a
Google account is required to appear on the leaderboard and to join real-time multiplayer
(creating/joining a room, quick match, or joining by code). Tap the sign-in button at the top of
the screen and sign in with your Google account to get started right away.

### How are leaderboard rankings calculated?

Rankings are sorted by a per-mode score that combines accuracy, typing speed, and completion
time, among other factors. Runs suspected of abuse may be reviewed and removed from the
leaderboard. Runs submitted while signed out are kept only as a personal practice record and are
never added to the leaderboard.

### How do I view or delete my data?

You can do this instantly as self-service, whether or not you're signed in, from the "My Data"
section at the bottom of the Privacy Policy (/privacy) page.

- Download my data — instantly downloads everything held about you as a JSON file
- Reset and delete my data — after a two-step confirmation, anonymizes your nickname and
  immediately deletes your leaderboard records and account mapping

### How do I report inappropriate content or a user?

If you spot inappropriate content in a nickname, room title, or chat, please send a report to the
contact address below with the time it happened, the room code or nickname involved, and a
description of what happened. We'll review it and take appropriate action.

### The game isn't working properly

Try updating your browser to the latest version and reloading the page. If the problem
continues, please let us know at the contact address below along with the browser and device
you're using.
